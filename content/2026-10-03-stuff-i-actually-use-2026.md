Not necessarily the best thing in every category. Just stuff I own, actually use, and would bother mentioning.

## Desk / Work Setup

- [Dell S2725QC — 27-inch 4K 120Hz USB-C Monitor](https://www.amazon.in/dp/B0F29RSLHP?th=1)  
  My main external monitor. 4K, 120Hz, USB-C, built-in speakers, and a properly adjustable stand.
- [MacBook Pro M4 Pro](https://www.apple.com/in/macbook-pro/)  
  Technically my office laptop, so I didn't buy this one. Still: GOAT-tier work machine and absolutely part of the setup.
- [IKEA SKÅDIS Pegboard](https://www.ikea.com/in/en/p/skadis-pegboard-white-80320804/)  
  Used as the organizer around my desk instead of having random stuff scattered everywhere.
- Custom 3D-printed SKÅDIS headphone mount  
  I was going to buy a headphone mount from Amazon, realized I could just have one printed for less, and got it made through [Dr.PrinT](https://drprint.in/). Very helpful guy, and the whole thing ended up cheaper than buying the mount I was looking at on Amazon.

## Audio

- [Sennheiser Momentum 4 Wireless Special Edition — Black/Copper](https://www.amazon.in/dp/B0CCRZPKR1?th=1)  
  Just to lock in every once in a while. Albeit I feel sad missing out on the Momentum 5.

  Great for road running, decent battery but not for audiophiles.

## Running / Cycling / Fitness

- [Garmin Forerunner 255 Music](https://www.amazon.in/dp/B0B4K8VB1D)  
  Still my main sports watch. GPS, running metrics, cycling support, music and enough battery life that I don't have to think about charging it constantly unlike my Apple Watch for which I have changed three straps in < 2 years. Spigen's straps left a lot to be desired.
- [Polar H9 Heart Rate Sensor](https://www.polar.com/in-en/sensors/h9-heart-rate-sensor)  
  Great for splits and training on the cycle trainer.
- [Zitel Protective Case for Garmin Forerunner 255 / 255 Music](https://www.amazon.in/dp/B0BRJHNQCF)  
  A must when you are dealing with a somewhat accident prone person like myself. Can skip.
- [Sportneer Magnetic Bike Trainer](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Sportneer+Magnetic+Bike+Trainer)  
  Dumb trainer in the best possible sense: put the bike on it, turn the resistance up and suffer. No subscriptions, no software stack and no pretending an indoor ride needs to be a SaaS product.
- [Van Rysel Long-Distance Triathlon Handlebar Extensions](https://www.decathlon.in/search?Ntt=Van%20Rysel%20aerobar)  
  Clip-on aero bars for my Giant Contend. Great for getting low, cutting through the wind and generally speeding around on my roadie.

## Clothing / Running Gear

- [ASICS Gel-Kayano](https://www.asics.co.in/collection/normal-cushioning-shoe/gel-kayano.html?page=1)  
  Deserves its share of glazing. I'm an overpronator, and this stability running shoe has carried me through 500 km so far with very little hassle.
- [Uniqlo DRY-EX T-Shirt](https://www.uniqlo.com/in/en/products/E483970-000/00)  
  These deserve unreasonable amounts of glazing. They feel lighter than air, don't become a miserable wet rag halfway through a run, and are one of the few running shirts I actively notice because of how little I notice wearing them.

## Gaming

- [Sony PlayStation 5 Slim — Disc Edition, 1TB](https://www.amazon.in/dp/B0CY5HVDS2?th=1)  
  The current PS5 Slim chassis with the disc drive. Probably the largest pure-entertainment purchase on this list. Got the last one before the price bump. Do I need it? Not really but excited to play GTA VI, so practicality be damned.
- [OIVO Dust Cover for PS5 Slim](https://www.amazon.in/dp/B0FL1Y5G3K)  
  A fitted dust cover compatible with both the Slim Disc and Digital models. A profoundly boring accessory that solves an equally boring problem but considering the amount of dust due to construction it became a must.
