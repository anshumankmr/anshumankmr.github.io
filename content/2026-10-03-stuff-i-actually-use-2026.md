## Running / Cycling / Fitness

- [Garmin Forerunner 255 Music](https://www.amazon.in/dp/B0B4K8VB1D)  
Still my main sports watch. GPS, running metrics, cycling support, music and enough battery life that I don't have to think about charging it constantly unlike my Apple Watch for which I have changed three straps in < 2 years *ugh*... I did kinda dislike the lack of touch on it but have come to get used to it. But yeah UI wise my Apple Watch is probably still too good.
- [Polar H9 Heart Rate Sensor](https://www.polar.com/in-en/sensors/h9-heart-rate-sensor)  
Great for running splits and training on the cycle trainer. Probably the gold standard though it did give me a nasty rash after my recent HM. Win some. Lose some I guess.
- [Zitel Protective Case for Garmin Forerunner 255 / 255 Music](https://www.amazon.in/dp/B0BRJHNQCF)  
A must when you are dealing with a somewhat accident prone person like myself.  Can't really say all that much besides that.
- [Sportneer Magnetic Bike Trainer](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Sportneer+Magnetic+Bike+Trainer)  
Dumb trainer in the best possible sense: put the bike on it, turn the resistance up and suffer. No subscriptions, no software stack and no pretending an indoor ride needs to be a SaaS product. The resistance could leave a bit to be desired, but it's good enough to hit Zone 4-5, which is fine by me. For now.
- [Van Rysel Long-Distance Triathlon Handlebar Extensions](https://www.decathlon.in/search?Ntt=Van%20Rysel%20aerobar)  
Clip-on aero bars for my Giant Contend. Great for getting low, cutting through the wind and generally speeding around on my roadie.

## Clothing / Running Gear

- [ASICS Gel-Kayano](https://www.asics.co.in/collection/normal-cushioning-shoe/gel-kayano.html?page=1)  
Deserves its share of glazing. I'm an overpronator, and this stability running shoe has carried me through 500 km so far with very little hassle, even on a wobbly knee that was already cooked before a riding accident, a cherry on the top that took me out of the game for some time.
- [Uniqlo DRY-EX T-Shirt](https://www.uniqlo.com/in/en/products/E483970-000/00)  
Real happy they came to Bangalore after their recent launch in Delhi. They feel lighter than air, don't become a miserable wet rag halfway through a run, and are one of the few running shirts I actively notice because of how little I notice wearing them. Also pretty great for doing lifts.

Solid Espresso machine, and decent at cappucinos too. A bit difficult to clean but I am guessing that is a ME problem rather than a De'Longhi issue.
