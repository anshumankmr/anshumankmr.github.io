I can tell you about Phan Boi Chau and the Vietnamese nationalist movement, because they were in my Class X exam. My Vijayanagara came from Amar Chitra Katha.

Then I went to Hampi, where my guide showed me what remains of Krishnadevaraya's private residence: stone foundations and enclosure walls. By around 1500 this was reportedly the second-largest city in the world after Beijing. It was sacked in 1565 and abandoned within a few years.

![Stone platform base and enclosure walls at Hampi, with open ground and a small shrine on a rocky hill in the distance](https://anshumankmr.github.io/media/8638162de165-hampi-royal-enclosure.jpg)

*Stone platform base and enclosure walls at Hampi. My guide identified this as what remains of Krishnadevaraya's private residence; only the foundations survive.*

The city fell, but the empire carried on from other capitals. Why the city itself never came back is the better question: the court had moved, and today the site is kept as a monument, with Hampi Bazaar's residents evicted for conservation.
